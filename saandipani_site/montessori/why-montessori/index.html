Why Montessori

Parents are often drawn to Montessori because it feels more human. Children move, choose, repeat, care for the environment, and learn in ways that feel purposeful rather than rushed.

The long-term advantage is not just academic readiness. It is self-direction, concentration, respect, vocabulary, grace, and confidence built from repeated real experiences.
