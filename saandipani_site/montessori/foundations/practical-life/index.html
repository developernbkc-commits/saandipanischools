Practical Life & Independence

Pouring, folding, buttoning, serving, cleaning, carrying, greeting, and caring for materials are not side activities. They are the work that prepares the hand, the will, and the mind.

In a complete Montessori concept school, practical life work also shapes culture. Children begin to see themselves as contributors to the classroom rather than passive recipients of instruction.
