The Four Foundations

Independence grows through practical routines, careful sequencing, and trust in the child’s ability to do more for themselves.

Discovery grows through sensorial materials, observation, repetition, and purposeful exploration. Expression grows through language, movement, art, communication, and the confidence to speak. Character grows through grace, courtesy, concentration, and care for the community.
