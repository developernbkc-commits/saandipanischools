Classroom Flow

A Montessori day is designed around the child’s need for uninterrupted engagement. Rather than constant interruption, the rhythm protects long periods of meaningful work and observation.

That creates a noticeably different experience for families: children talk about what they did, what they chose, what they practised, and how they helped, not only what they were told to finish.
