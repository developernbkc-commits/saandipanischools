School experience

The Foundational Years are where the school’s Montessori identity becomes most visible and most meaningful for children and families.

This stage is designed to honour the pace and potential of early childhood. Beautifully prepared spaces, practical independence, and rich sensorial experiences help children learn with joy and ownership.

Families are invited into a picture of school life that feels reassuring and substantial: not only academic beginnings, but also movement, expression, confidence, and a strong sense of belonging.
