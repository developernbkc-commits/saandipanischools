School experience

A parent-friendly picture of what children aged 3 to 6 may experience in a complete Montessori beginning.

The ages 3 to 6 stage is a remarkable period of absorption, imitation, order, and emerging capability. Montessori meets that stage with materials and routines that feel both freeing and purposeful.

Children in this age range benefit from consistency, beauty, real responsibility, and work that they can repeat until confidence becomes visible in everyday life.
