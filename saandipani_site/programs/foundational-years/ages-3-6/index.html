Ages 3 to 6 Program

At this stage, children need movement, language, order, repetition, and meaningful choice. The classroom responds with a carefully prepared range of practical life, sensorial, language, mathematics, art, music, movement, and grace-and-courtesy experiences.

For families, the outcome is visible in the child’s growing ability to focus, speak clearly, care for materials, complete routines, and take pride in independent work.
