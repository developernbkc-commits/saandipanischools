About Saandipani

Saandipani is being shaped as a premium yet welcoming Montessori-led school for modern families in Poranki and the larger Vijayawada growth corridor.

The brand combines warmth, elegance, and clarity: children are respected, parents are partnered with, and every decision is intended to strengthen the learning environment rather than distract from it.
