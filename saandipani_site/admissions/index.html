Families considering a school need more than enthusiasm. They need clarity, relevance, and the confidence that their questions will be answered well. Saandipani’s admissions approach is built around those principles.

Whether a parent prefers to read first, enquire online, or speak directly with the admissions desk, the school should feel approachable and well organised from the first interaction.
