Montessori at Home

At home, families can begin with very small shifts: a reachable shelf, a predictable routine, a jug for pouring water, a place for shoes, a basket for laundry, a cloth for wiping spills, and time for the child to try before an adult steps in.

These are not cosmetic ideas. They reinforce the same habits of independence, order, movement, and care that make Montessori environments so powerful in school.
